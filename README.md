# KG Machinery Gateway

Build a modern, professional, responsive website for an Ethiopian company named:



“Kefyalew Girma Machinery Rental & Land Transport”



The company rents heavy construction equipment and provides land transportation services in Ethiopia. The brand should feel reliable, strong, modern, premium, and practical—not overly decorative.



Design direction:

- Take general inspiration from high-quality machinery-rental platforms: clean layout, large construction photography, strong typography, clear navigation, and easy quotation actions.

- Do not copy any existing website.

- Use a warm Ethiopian-inspired palette: construction yellow/gold as the main accent, deep forest green, earth brown, off-white/cream backgrounds, and charcoal text.

- Use a modern bold sans-serif font such as Plus Jakarta Sans, Manrope, or Inter.

- Make the site mobile responsive and accessible.



Hero section:

- Use a high-quality full-width background image of an active construction site with heavy equipment, such as excavators, loaders, dump trucks, and road construction activity.

- Apply a subtle dark overlay so text stays readable.

- At the center of the hero, display only the company name in a large, smooth horizontal moving animation.

- The animation should move gently from left to right, feel premium and continuous, and respect reduced-motion settings.

- Rotate or continuously show the company name in these three languages:

  1. Amharic: ከፍያለው ግርማ ማሽነሪ ኪራይ እና የየብስ ትራንስፖርት ተዛማጅ ስራዋች

  2. English: Kefyalew Girma Machinery Rental & Land Transport

  3. Chinese: 克菲亚勒夫·吉尔马机械租赁与陆路运输

- Do not place borders, lines, boxes, slogans, or unnecessary text around the moving company name.

- Include a clear “Request a Quote” call-to-action in the navigation or beneath the hero.



Pages / sections:

1. Home

2. Our Fleet

   - Excavators

   - Wheel Loaders

   - Dump Trucks

   - Lowbed Transport

3. Land Transportation

4. About Us

5. Contact / Request a Quote



Home page structure:

- Top utility bar with Ethiopia location and phone number placeholder

- Clean main navigation with company logo initials “KG”

- Hero section described above

- Quick quote form: equipment type, project location, rental dates, request quote button

- “Our Fleet” section with premium image cards for Excavators, Wheel Loaders, Dump Trucks, and Lowbed Transport

- Why choose us section with benefits: reliable equipment, professional support, flexible rental, dependable transport

- Strong final CTA section: “Need machinery for your next project?”

- Professional footer with company name, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, phone, email, and navigation links



UI requirements:

- Spacious layout and excellent visual hierarchy

- Rounded corners only where appropriate; avoid excessive rounded UI

- Smooth hover states and subtle motion only

- Cards should use real machinery photography, not cartoons

- Buttons should be large, clear, and easy to tap on mobile

- Build a polished, production-quality landing page first, then make all navigation and quote form interactions work.
